About Me

My interests lie in alternative computational models for artificial intelligence, mass social patterns, chaotic and emergent systems, and linguistics. My current research is focused on novel ways of scaling deep neural networks through neurologically inspired modularity.

Questions such as: How can we reduce parameter space, increase interpretability, and create self-similar task divisions like the brain?

I was a 2024 Foresight Institute Fellow in Neurotechnology. You can find the press release on the Foresight Institute's website.

I was also the Co-Founder of Myelin Neurotech, a nonprofit working on neurotechnology research. You can find our work on the Myelin Neurotech page. I previously worked at the Laboratory for Integrated Brain Imaging (UofM) as a NeuroAI researcher until May 2026.

I am currently employed at the Sripada Lab at the University of Michigan, and I also pursue independent work in computation and AI. I direct HuronARC, a sovereign lab built to give scientists the freedom and infrastructure to pursue breakthrough work, free from the rigid oversight of public markets and the fragmentation of modern academia.
