Scientific Bio

My interests lie in alternative computational models for artificial intelligence, mass social patterns, chaotic and emergent systems, and linguistics. My research was focused on novel ways of scaling deep neural networks through neurologically inspired modularity.

Questions such as: How can we reduce parameter space, increase interpretability, and create self-similar task divisions like the brain?

I received bachelor's degrees in Computational Cognitive Science and Music Composition from the University of Michigan, with minors in Computer Science and Complex Systems. I was awarded the Sam Epstein Award for Contributions to Theoretical Cognitive Science by the Weinberg Institute for Cognitive Science, for engagement with foundational, theoretical, and conceptual issues in the field.

I was a 2024 Foresight Institute Fellow in Neurotechnology. You can find the press release on the Foresight Institute's website.

I was also the Co-Founder of Myelin Neurotech, a nonprofit working on neurotechnology research. I previously worked at the Laboratory for Integrated Brain Imaging (UofM) as a NeuroAI researcher until May 2026.

I am currently a staff researcher at the Sripada Lab at the University of Michigan, where I also pursue independent work in computation and AI. I also direct HuronARC, a sovereign lab built to give scientists the freedom and infrastructure to pursue breakthrough or economically meaningful work, free from the rigid oversight of public markets and the fragmentation of modern academia.
